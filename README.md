# Mighty PDF Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Mighty PDF is developed by Cristian Zeni ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app or contact us for support.

## Our approach to privacy

Mighty PDF processes your documents on your iPhone. We do not operate a server that receives your documents, require an app account, include advertising, or use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell your personal information.

Local processing and storage are necessary to provide the app's features. This is different from collecting your documents or usage information on our servers.

## Documents, scans, and text recognition

PDF editing, compression, conversion, camera scanning, and optical character recognition (OCR) take place on your device. The app does not upload your documents, scanned images, extracted text, or document passwords to us or to a remote processing service.

Saved documents are stored in the app's local Documents folder, accessible through **Files > On My iPhone > Mighty PDF**. You decide which documents to import, create, save, share, move, or delete.

The app also creates local working files while processing documents. OCR text and exported JPEG or PNG files are prepared temporarily for sharing; you must use the sharing options to save them elsewhere. Temporary operation files are removed when the operation's workspace is cleaned up, normally when you close or cancel the workflow.

Document passwords are used to perform the requested operation and are not saved as app preferences or credentials.

## Camera, photos, and file access

Mighty PDF requests camera permission when you use document scanning. You can change this permission in iOS Settings. Without camera access, camera scanning is unavailable; other tools remain available.

When you import photos or files, the app uses the items you select through the system's selection interfaces to perform the requested operation.

## Preferences and the daily usage counter

The app stores settings and onboarding preferences locally. To enforce the free daily allowance, it stores a date and a count of completed free operations in the device's Keychain. This counter contains no document contents or document names and is not sent to us.

Keychain information may remain after you uninstall and reinstall the app. Deleting your documents does not reset the daily usage counter or change your purchases.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes payments for monthly and annual subscriptions and the one-time Lifetime purchase. Mighty PDF uses Apple's StoreKit framework to check your purchase entitlement and unlock Pro features.

We do not receive your payment card details. Apple handles purchase information under its own terms and [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can manage subscriptions through your Apple Account settings.

## Sharing and device backups

Mighty PDF does not provide its own iCloud document synchronization. If you choose to share or copy a file to another app, cloud storage provider, or recipient, that destination handles the file under its own policies.

Depending on your device settings, local app data may be included in backups managed by Apple or in backups you create yourself. You control those backups and any copies stored outside Mighty PDF.

## Support communications

If you email **mightypdf@protonmail.com**, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve support issues. Our email provider, Proton Mail, processes these communications as part of delivering the email service.

Please avoid sending sensitive documents unless they are necessary for your request. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and any related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request access to or deletion of information you have provided, subject to applicable legal requirements.

## Deleting your information

You can delete individual local documents in Mighty PDF or the Files app, or use **Settings > Delete all local documents** in Mighty PDF. Deleting a local file does not delete copies you previously shared or saved elsewhere, or copies in existing backups.

We cannot access, retrieve, or delete documents stored only on your device. Manage external copies and backups through the relevant apps or services. For information you have sent to us by email, contact the support address below.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or information-handling practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

# Mighty PDF Support

For any technical support request, question, or issue with Mighty PDF, contact the app's developer, **Cristian Zeni**, by email:

**[mightypdf@protonmail.com](mailto:mightypdf@protonmail.com)**

## How to request help

Describe the issue and, if possible, include:

- Your iPhone model and iOS version.
- The Mighty PDF version, shown in the app's Settings.
- The steps that led to the issue and what you expected to happen.
- Any error message or a screenshot that helps explain the problem.

Please do not include passwords, payment details, or sensitive documents. If you attach a screenshot, hide any personal or confidential information first.

You can also reach this email address from **Settings > Contact support** in Mighty PDF.
